How it works.

Challenge host

github.com/orhun ↗
  1. Choose push-ups, dips or a timed dip bar hold.
  2. Complete one supervised attempt with a booth team member.
  3. Add your name and verified score to the live leaderboard.
  4. Come back for the daily winners. They are announced at the booth and online.
Daily prizes: JetBrains All Products Pack licenses, JetBrains merch, participation stickers and the deliberately tiny 1 kg Developer Dumbbell.

Participation is voluntary. Equipment is supervised and demonstrations are provided. Stop immediately if you feel pain or discomfort.
